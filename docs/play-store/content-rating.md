# Content rating and target audience

## Content rating questionnaire (IARC)

Category: **Utility, Productivity, Communication or other** (not a game, not social media in the IARC sense because users only talk to their own company's phone system and a closed address book).

| Question | Answer |
| --- | --- |
| Violence, blood, fear, sexual content, profanity, drugs, gambling, hate, ... | **No** to all |
| Does the app allow users to interact or exchange content with each other? | **Yes**, voice calls and voicemail between members of a company and external callers (users are not anonymous strangers: a company administrator controls access) |
| Does the app share the user's location with other users? | **No** |
| Does the app allow purchases of digital goods? | **No** |
| Does the app contain unrestricted web access or user-generated content that is shown to others? | **No** |

Expected rating: **PEGI 3 / Everyone** (the user-interaction note may show "Users interact"). Answer the questionnaire again yourself on the day and read the result; do not rely on this table.

## Target audience and content

- Target age group: **18 and over** (business tool). Select only "18+" (do not tick any group under 13, which would pull in the Families policy).
- App is not designed for children, no appeal to children (no cartoon characters, no game elements).
- Contains ads: **No**.
- App category: Business. Tags: Business, Communication tools.
- News app / COVID-19 / government / health apps declarations: **No**.
